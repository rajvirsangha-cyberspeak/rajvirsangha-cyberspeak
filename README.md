## About Me

I am a cybersecurity professional and mentor focused on **Microsoft cloud security, identity, endpoint security, incident response, security architecture, and compliance**.

My work centers on a practical principle:

> **Cybersecurity skill should be demonstrated, not merely claimed.**

I care deeply about helping early-career professionals move beyond passive learning, build credible technical evidence, and communicate their work with clarity.

I founded **Cyber Speak** to make that process more structured, practical, and accountable.

---

## Cyber Speak

**Cyber Speak** is a practical cybersecurity mentorship community for people building toward their first or next cybersecurity role.

The community focuses on:

- 1:1 mentorship
- Resume and LinkedIn positioning
- Interview preparation
- Certification strategy
- GitHub portfolio development
- Practical cybersecurity projects
- Career accountability
- Technical communication

The objective is simple:

**Build skills. Produce evidence. Explain your work.**

## How I Think About Cybersecurity

A strong cybersecurity professional should be able to do more than identify the right answer.

They should be able to:

- Build the environment
- Investigate the evidence
- Understand the business impact
- Make a defensible decision
- Document the work
- Explain the reasoning

That is the standard I apply to my own work and the standard I teach through Cyber Speak.
